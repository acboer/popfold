## Popfold 0.4.0: tidy up your folders

### New
- **Reorder apps by dragging**: inside an open folder, drag an app to a new spot. The 2×2 preview, the taskbar icon and the jump list follow the new order.
- **Move apps between folders**: drag an app out of the open panel and drop it onto another folder on the desktop.
- **Update notices**: once a day Popfold asks GitHub whether a new version is out and lets you know in the tray. Click the notice to open the download page. You can also use **Check for updates** in the tray menu, or turn the notices off in *Options*. Popfold only reads the public release list: nothing about you is sent, and nothing is downloaded on its own.

### Improved
- *Options* now shows the full version number (for example 0.4.0).

### Install
Download **Popfold-Setup-0.4.0.exe** below and run it. Installing over 0.3.0 keeps your folders. Requires Windows 10 (1809) or 11, 64-bit.

Popfold isn't digitally signed yet, so Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"*: click **More info** → **Run anyway**.
