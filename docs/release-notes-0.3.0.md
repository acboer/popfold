## Popfold 0.3.0: first public release 🎉

Android/iOS-style folders for the Windows desktop and taskbar.

### Highlights
- **Folders on the desktop** with a 2×2 preview of their apps. Click to open a compact panel, click an app to launch it.
- **Drag and drop** programs, shortcuts, files, folders and website links onto a folder.
- **Pin folders to the taskbar**: the panel opens right above the taskbar, and right-clicking the pinned icon lists its apps. Folders can also live *only* on the taskbar.
- **New > Popfold** in the desktop right-click menu (shows up after one restart).
- **Multi-select** with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+click or a selection box; <kbd>Delete</kbd> removes the selected folders.
- **Glass look** that blends with your wallpaper, adjustable transparency, light and dark styles.
- **English and Portuguese**, picked automatically.

### Install
Download **Popfold-Setup-0.3.0.exe** below and run it. Requires Windows 10 (1809) or 11, 64-bit. No other downloads needed.

Popfold isn't digitally signed yet, so Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"*: click **More info** → **Run anyway**.
